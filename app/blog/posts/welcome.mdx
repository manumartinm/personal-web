# Welcome to My Blog

Welcome to my personal blog! This is where I share my thoughts, experiences, and learnings about various topics that interest me.

## What to Expect

In this blog, I'll be writing about:

- **Development**: Web development, software engineering, and technical insights
- **Data Science**: My journey studying Data Science and Mathematics
- **Research**: Updates on my research in Drug Design & Genetics at VRAIN
- **Entrepreneurship**: Lessons learned from building startups and products
- **Personal**: Thoughts on life, sports, and other interests

## About Me

I'm Manuel, a Software Engineer at Tinybird with experience in Marketing, Development, Data Engineering, and Entrepreneurship since 2015. I previously founded Keytrends, leading the Data & AI team, and worked as a consultant on various projects.

Currently, I'm pursuing dual Bachelor's degrees in Data Science (UPV Valencia) and Mathematics (VIU), while conducting research in Drug Design & Genetics at VRAIN and advising startups at the intersection of AI and Marketing.

Thanks for reading, and I hope you enjoy the content!
